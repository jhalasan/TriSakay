2a · REDESIGNPassenger — Complaints & case tracker

The tab stops being one long form with past cases buried under the submit button. It opens on the same navy band as History and Profile, leads with Report a problem, and lists your cases with a three-segment progress bar so status reads without opening anything. Filing splits into two short steps — pick the trip and a category tile, then describe it with subject suggestions — and ends on a receipt with the reference number. The tracker puts the current stage in a “Now” card, dates every stage, and keeps the case details and evidence on the same page. Same tokens as 1a: navy for action, green for done, 16px fields, 38px icon tiles.
