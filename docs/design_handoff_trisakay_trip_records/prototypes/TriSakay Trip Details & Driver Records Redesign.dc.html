3a · REDESIGNRate driver (passenger) & My ratings (driver)

Verdict — needs it. In rate-driver.tsx: (1) Skip for now only appears after a submit error, so a passenger who doesn't want to rate has no way out except the system back gesture; (2) all 8 tags show at once, so a 5-star rider is offered "Rude" and "Unsafe driving"; (3) the stars have no meaning label and the page is vertically centred, pushing Submit below the keyboard; (4) a 1–2 star ride has no route to PSO even though that's the moment it matters. In ratings.tsx: the distribution bars have no numbers, and the tags passengers pick are never shown to the driver, so "3.8" gives them nothing to act on.

Redesign. Skip is always in the header. Stars are 44px with a word under them (Terrible → Excellent). Tags follow the score: 4–5 stars shows the four positive RATING_TAGS, 1–3 shows the four negative ones, same enum. Comment is collapsed until tapped on high scores and open on low ones. At ≤2 stars a Report to PSO card links to /complaints/new?rideRequestId=…. The driver side labels every bar with its share, adds "What passengers mention" (tag counts over the loaded 50), shows tags on each rating card, and puts Report unfair rating on low ones (driver complaints already has low_rating). One data change: useRatingsStore must select the existing tags column.
