NEWDriver — Gates & account states

Everything standing between logging in and going online. Each gate is one column, one icon, one action: consent, location, verification, suspension. The unsubmitted case is the only gate with a form, because it is the only one the driver can actually clear from the phone — the rest end at Refresh status or a visit to the PSO office. Red is reserved for suspension.

The request card is the driver app's most important object, so it keeps its dashboard form exactly: green payment header with the fare, pickup and drop-off on one connector rail, Decline left and Accept right. The active trip inherits the passenger app's map-first system — floating white sheet on radius 26 over the route — with one card per passenger, because mid-trip pickup means there can be more than one.
