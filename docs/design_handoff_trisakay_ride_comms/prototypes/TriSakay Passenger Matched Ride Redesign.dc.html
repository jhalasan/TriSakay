REDESIGN Passenger — Matched ride (booking/trip)
What's hard today
The whole navy sheet looks the same whether the driver is 4 minutes away, already waiting, or driving. ETA, plate, seats and fare all have the same weight, so the plate (the thing you need to find the trike) doesn't stand out. A full-width red SOS bar is the biggest thing on screen. Cancel is a small underlined link. There's no way to reach the driver, and the screen says so.
What changes
This mirrors the driver's accepted-ride screen. A white floating sheet leads with one big status: the ETA, "Your driver is here", or time to drop-off. A three-step progress bar sits under it. The driver strip gives the plate its own tag and has Call and Message buttons. The route and fare stay visible. SOS moves to the same hold button on the map, and Cancel becomes a proper button that only shows before pickup. Use the switcher under 5a to step through.
