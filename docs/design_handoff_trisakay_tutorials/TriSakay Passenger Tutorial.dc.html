NEWPassenger — In-app tutorial (guided walkthrough)

Ten coach marks across six screens, not one. The tour advances the screen behind the dim as you tap Next, so the copy always points at the thing you would really tap: home (header, Request a Tricycle, Saved places, tab bar), a real booking (pickup and destination, fare confirmation, live trip with Emergency SOS), and then the complaint path this app exists for — filing against a specific trip, attaching evidence, and tracking the case to a resolution. Skip is on every step; Restart replays it from Settings → Help.
