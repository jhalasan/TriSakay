Findings, fixed in priority order:
- **Critical (would strand a driver):** (1) `trip/active.tsx`'s passenger-card list had no scroll container — confirmed live, a 3rd simultaneous passenger's card rendered off the top of the viewport (`MapOverlaySheet` grows unbounded, anchored to the bottom) with no way to scroll to it or reach its Cancel/Complete buttons.
